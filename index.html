Radish: Recipes & Planning

Collect recipes from anywhere, plan the week, and shop for it, together with the people you cook with.

Radish is an app for iPhone and iPad. Save recipes from Instagram, YouTube and food blogs, organize them with tags, build a weekly meal plan, and turn it into a shopping list. Everyone in your household shares one recipe library, one plan and one list, each on their own Apple ID.
