Support

Radish is made by two people, and we read everything you send.

Email us: support@getradishrecipes.com

We usually reply within a few days.

Send feedback from the app

The fastest way to reach us is inside the app:

  • Shake your phone on any screen, or
  • open Settings → Send feedback.

Tell us what went wrong or what you wish the app did. Your message arrives with the screen you were on, which helps us find the problem.

Common questions

How do I save a recipe from Instagram, YouTube or a website?
Open the post, video or page, tap Share, and choose Radish. You can also paste a link inside the app with Import from URL on the Recipes tab. Check the result before saving; you can edit anything.

How do I share recipes with my household?
Open Settings, go to your household, and tap Invite someone. Each person signs in with their own Apple ID. Once they accept, you share one recipe library, one meal plan and one shopping list.

Why did an AI feature say it was busy or that I reached a limit?
Importing, planning and recipe variations use an AI service that we pay for, so there is a daily limit per person. It resets the next day. Everything else in the app keeps working.

My changes are not showing up on another device.
Make sure both devices are online, then close and reopen the app on each. If they still differ, send feedback from the app and tell us which recipe or list is affected.

Delete your account

Open Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone. If you share a household, the app asks whether the recipes you shared should stay with the household. The privacy policy explains exactly what is removed.

If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.
