Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Radish is a recipe, meal planning and shopping list app made by a two-person team. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and how to delete it.

The short version: your data is yours. We do not sell it, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. What you put in Radish is visible only to you and the household members you invite. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

What we store

  • Your account. Sign in with Apple is the only way to sign in. We receive the identifier Apple gives us and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only get the relay address. We never see your Apple password.
  • Your recipes. Titles, ingredients, steps, tags, source links, photos you attach, and the notes and variations you create.
  • Your plans, lists and cooking feedback. Meal plans, shopping lists, ratings, notes and weekly reflections.
  • Your household. Who you have invited and who has joined.
  • Usage and diagnostics. Basic events such as "recipe imported" or "plan generated", error reports, and a count of the AI requests you make each day. These are tied to your account so we can fix problems and enforce daily limits.
  • Feedback you send. What you wrote and which screen you were on.

The app asks for access to your photos only when you choose to attach a photo to a recipe. It does not access your contacts, location, microphone or anything else outside what you put in it.

Who can see your content

Your content is stored in a database we operate. Access rules in that database allow only you, and the household members you have invited, to read it.

When you share a recipe with someone outside your household, the app sends them a copy through the share sheet. They do not get access to your account or your library.

The two of us can access stored data when we need to in order to fix a problem or answer a support request.

AI features

Importing a recipe from a link, refining a meal plan, and creating a variation from your notes use models from OpenAI. When you use one of these features, the recipe text, web page content or notes involved are sent to OpenAI through a server we control. When you save or edit a recipe, its text is also sent the same way to build a search index for your library. We do not send your name, email address or account identifier with these requests. OpenAI processes this content on our behalf and, under its API terms, does not use it to train its models.

When you import from a link, the app fetches that page from your device, the same way a web browser would.

Companies that process data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in service and photo storage.
  • OpenAI provides the AI models described above.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and distributes the app. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple also sends us anonymous crash reports.

These companies may process data in the United States and other countries. We do not use any other analytics, advertising or tracking services.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with anyone for advertising or marketing.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual recipes, plans and lists in the app at any time.

To delete your account, open Settings → Account → Delete account. Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone.

When you delete your account, Radish deletes your profile, your sign-in, your recipes, the meal plans you created, your notes and feedback, and your usage data. We also revoke the app's connection to your Apple ID.

If you share a household that still has other members, the app gives you two choices before deleting:

  • Keep the recipes you shared with your household. If you choose this, those recipes and their photos stay with the household without your name.
  • Leave your notes and cook feedback with your household. If you choose this, your notes and feedback on recipes that stay are kept and shown as "Former member".

If you turn both off, or you do not share a household, everything is deleted.

Removing the app from your device does not delete your account. If you cannot use the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it within 30 days.

Backup copies held by our hosting provider expire on their own schedule, within 30 days of deletion.

How long we keep data

We keep your content and account for as long as your account exists. When you delete something, or your account, it is removed as described above.

Children

Radish is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and the effective date, and note the change in the app's release notes.
